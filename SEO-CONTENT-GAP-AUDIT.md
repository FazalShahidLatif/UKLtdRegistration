# SEO Content Gap Audit

Date: 2026-09-04

## Scope

This audit reviewed the public positioning and visible content themes of five UK formation and business-support competitors:

- [1st Formations](https://www.1stformations.co.uk/)
- [Icon Offices](https://www.iconoffices.co.uk/)
- [Your Company Formations](https://www.yourcompanyformations.co.uk/)
- [Companies Made Simple](https://www.companiesmadesimple.com/)
- [Quality Company Formations](https://www.qualitycompanyformations.co.uk/)

The review used publicly visible home, package, service, FAQ, and blog content. It is a directional content audit, not a claim about rankings or private analytics.

## Repeated competitor themes

1. Direct Companies House filing versus agent support.
2. Package comparison with the government fee separated from the service fee.
3. Non-resident setup, address privacy, identity checks, and banking choices.
4. Post-formation work: confirmation statements, accounts, Corporation Tax, VAT, and company changes.
5. Practical checklists, FAQs, bank partner pages, and clear package CTAs.
6. Office products linked to formation, mail handling, registered office, and director service address.

## Existing site strengths

UK LTD Registration already has strong regional, export, banking, VAT, ACSP, address, and company-name content. The main gap was a neutral decision guide that explains when direct filing is enough and when paid support creates value. A second gap was a single pre-application checklist for overseas founders that joins identity, address, SIC, banking, tax, and first filing tasks.

## New content plan

### Article 1

- Slug: `companies-house-vs-formation-agent-uk-2026`
- Intent: informational, commercial investigation, navigational, and transactional.
- Gap covered: direct filing versus agent support, full cost questions, address needs, banking limits, and post-formation duties.
- CTA path: `/pricing`, `/register-a-limited-company-uk`, and `/contact`.
- Scheduled release: 2026-09-08 at 09:00 Europe/London.

### Article 2

- Slug: `non-resident-uk-company-setup-checklist-2026`
- Intent: informational, commercial investigation, navigational, and transactional.
- Gap covered: one plain-English checklist for overseas founders before payment or submission.
- CTA path: `/company-name-check`, `/services/virtual-office`, `/register-a-limited-company-uk`, and `/contact`.
- Scheduled release: 2026-09-15 at 09:00 Europe/London.

## White-hat implementation

The posts use original explanations, official GOV.UK references, accurate caveats about bank approval and processing time, FAQ structured data, canonical internal links, short metadata, and runtime publication filtering. No competitor text, paid links, doorway pages, hidden text, or guaranteed ranking claims were added.
