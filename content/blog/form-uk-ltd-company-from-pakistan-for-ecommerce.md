## Step 2: Choose Your Company Name and Check Availability

Your company name must:

- Be unique — no other UK company can have the same name
- Not be offensive or mislead customers
- Not contain restricted words (e.g., "Bank", "Government", "Trust") unless you have proper licensing
- End with "Limited" or "Ltd" (for public companies)

## Step 4: Required Details for London Incorporation

When you file with Companies House, you'll need:

- **Company name** (must be unique)
- **Registered office address** (UK)
- **SIC codes** (Standard Industrial Classification) — tell Companies House what your business does
- **Director details** (name, date of birth, country of residence, occupation)
- **Shareholder details** (name, address, number of shares)
- **Person with significant control (PSC)** — you, if you own more than 25% of the company
- **UK electronic mail address** (for official communications)

> ✅ You can be your own director. If you need one, we can appoint a licensed director.

## Step 6: Tax Setup and Compliance

After incorporation, you must:

1. **Register for Corporation Tax** — within 3 months of starting to trade
2. **File annual accounts** — with Companies House
3. **File annual confirmation statement** — with Companies House
4. **Register for VAT** — if your turnover exceeds £90,000 (2026 threshold)
5. **File Corporation Tax returns** — HMRC requires this annually

**UK LTD tax rate 2026:** 19% (£0–£50,000), 25% (over £250,000)
