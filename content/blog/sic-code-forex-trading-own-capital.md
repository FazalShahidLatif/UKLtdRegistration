# UK SIC Code for Forex Trading Own Capital — 2026 Guide

If you're a non-resident or UK resident trading forex (currency) personally, you need to choose the correct **SIC code** (Standard Industrial Classification) for your UK Ltd company. HMRC uses these codes to classify your income source for tax purposes.

This guide explains the right SIC code for forex trading with your own capital, and what happens if you use the wrong one.

---

## What Is a SIC Code?

SIC codes (Standard Industrial Classification) are 2-digit numbers that describe what your company does. Companies House uses them to classify your business. HMRC uses them to classify your income source for tax purposes.

Your SIC code tells HMRC:

- What industry your business operates in
- What type of income your company generates
- What tax rules apply to your trading activity

---

## SIC Codes for Forex Trading

| SIC Code | Description | When to Use |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **66112** | Financial Instrument Trading | Forex trading, currency trading, financial trading |
| **64110** | Auxiliary Finance | Money changing, financial broker, currency exchange |
| **66110** | Investment Buying/Selling | Buying and selling financial instruments |
| **46912** | Foreign Currency Dealing | Money exchange business (if you're actually dealing in FX) |

### Best SIC Codes for Forex Trading

| Trading Activity | Recommended SIC Code |
|------------------|---------------------|
| **Trading forex with your own capital** | **66112** |
| Currency exchange/mid-market dealing | 64110 |
| Currency arbitrage | 66112 |
| Buying/selling foreign currency as investment | 66110 |

## Why SIC Code Matters for Forex Traders

Your SIC code determines:

1. **How HMRC classifies your income** — trading income vs. investment income
2. **What tax applies** — Capital Gains Tax vs. Income Tax vs. Corporation Tax
3. **What HMRC expects from you** — trading records, tax returns, self-assessment
4. **Whether you need to register for VAT** — high-value financial services may trigger VAT relevance

---

## Is Forex Trading Your Own Capital "Business"?

For tax purposes, you must decide whether your trading activity is:

### 1. Business Trading (Favorable)

If you regularly trade forex with your own capital, HMRC may treat this as a **business activity**. Benefits:

- Profits taxed under **Corporation Tax** (not Income Tax)
- You can claim trading expenses (platform fees, data subscriptions, etc.)
- Loss relief may be available
- You can be a UK Ltd director with the company as the trading vehicle

### 2. Investment Activity (Less Favorable)

If HMRC classifies your activity as **investment**, you may face:

- Capital Gains Tax (not Corporation Tax)
- Fewer expense deductions
- No loss relief on personal losses
- Higher avoidance risk

> ✅ If you trade forex regularly with your own capital, **66112 (Financial Instrument Trading)** is the correct SIC code.

## What If I Use the Wrong SIC Code?

Using the wrong SIC code can cause problems:

- **HMRC may reclassify your income** — you could be assessed for higher tax
- **Claiming wrong expenses** — if HMRC says your activity is "investment", you can't claim trading expenses
- **Non-resident issues** — non-residents trading without proper SIC codes risk tax notices
- **Late filing penalties** — if your filing doesn't match HMRC records
