## Step 1: Check Your Company Name

Your company name must be:

- Unique (no other Company House registration)
- Not offensive or misleading
- Not containing restricted words (unless licensed)
- Ending with "Limited" or "Ltd" (for a private limited company)

**How to check:**
1. Go to [Companies House website](https://find-and-apply.company-registration.service.gov.uk/)
