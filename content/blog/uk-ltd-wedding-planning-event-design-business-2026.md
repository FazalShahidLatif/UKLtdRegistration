# UK LTD for Wedding Planning & Event Design Business: Start a Wedding Planner, Event Designer, Bridal Styling, Wedding Coordination, Luxury Wedding Services, or Full-Service Event Planning Company in 2026

If you are a wedding planner, event designer, bridal stylist, wedding coordinator, event stylist, luxury wedding professional, or anyone who wants to start or run a wedding planning and event design business with a professional UK entity — whether you are based in London or operating from Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nigeria, UAE, Turkey, China, Vietnam, Brazil, or anywhere in the world — a UK LTD is the legal structure that lets you run a wedding planning company, event design business, bridal styling service, wedding coordination service, luxury wedding business, or full-service event planning company under one company, with limited liability, professional credibility, proper VAT handling, insurance, client contracts, and multi-currency banking.

This guide covers what a UK LTD means for wedding planning and event design businesses.

## Why a UK LTD for Wedding Planning & Event Design Business

Wedding planning and event design businesses involve client relationships, supplier networks, client contracts and deposits, liability (cancellation, supplier failure, event issues), insurance, travel (venue sourcing, supplier meetings, destination weddings), multi-currency transactions (international couples, destination weddings abroad, suppliers in different currencies), and professional credibility. A UK LTD gives these businesses:

| What a wedding planning/event design business without a UK LTD faces | What a UK LTD gives |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| Personal liability for event issues, supplier failure, cancellation, client disputes, property damage, public liability at events | UK LTD = limited liability — wedding/event business risks sit with the company |
| Operating under personal name or informal structure — less professional credibility with clients (couples, families), venues, suppliers, partners | UK LTD = registered company — professional credibility with couples, families, venues, suppliers, partners |
| Difficulty opening business bank accounts for planning/service income | UK LTD = recognised entity for business banking — planning fees, coordination fees, event design fees, styling fees, etc. |
| Multi-currency transactions — international couples, destination weddings abroad, suppliers in different currencies | UK LTD multi-currency business account (Wise/Revolut) handles wedding/event income and expenses in multiple currencies |
| VAT on wedding planning and event design services — planning, coordination, design, styling, etc. | UK LTD VAT registration gives proper VAT framework — wedding planning/design services generally standard-rated (20%) |
| Client contracts and deposits — wedding planning contracts, deposits, cancellation policies, liability, supplier contracts | UK LTD = structure for client agreements — proper wedding planning contracts, deposits, cancellation policies, liability clauses, supplier agreements |
| Insurance — wedding insurance, public liability, professional indemnity, equipment, cancellation insurance, etc. | UK LTD = structure for insurance — proper insurance for wedding/event business |
| Supplier network — venues, caterers, florists, photographers, musicians, etc. — building and managing supplier relationships | UK LTD = structure for supplier relationships — formal supplier agreements, preferred supplier lists, supplier management |
| Travel — venue sourcing, supplier meetings, wedding fairs, destination weddings, event site visits | UK LTD = structure for travel arrangements, travel expenses, travel insurance |
| Scaling — adding planners, designers, services, locations, destination weddings, event types | UK LTD = scalable base — add planners, designers, services, locations, destination weddings, event types under one company |
| Difficulty building brand trust and client confidence | UK LTD brand — professional trading name, trust signals, credibility with couples, venues, suppliers |

**For non-resident wedding planners and event designers**, a UK LTD is the bridge that lets you run a wedding planning and event design business in the UK with a registered UK company — serving UK couples, international couples, destination weddings, or both — with a professional UK entity.

## Step 2: Building Your Supplier Network — Venues, Caterers, Florists, Photographers, etc.

A wedding planner/event designer's business depends heavily on a strong supplier network — venues, caterers, florists, photographers, musicians, hair/makeup artists, bakeries, stationers, etc. Building and managing this network is core to the business.

**Key supplier categories:**

| Supplier | Role | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| **Venues** | Wedding/event venues — ceremony venues, reception venues, barns, hotels, estates, gardens, marquees, etc. | Venue sourcing, venue visits, venue relationships, venue availability, venue contracts, venue pricing, venue suitability for different weddings/events. Venues are central to wedding planning — the venue sets the location, capacity, style, and often includes catering/catering restrictions, accommodation, etc. |
| **Caterers** | Catering for weddings/events — food and beverage, menus, service, etc. | Caterer sourcing, caterer relationships, menu development, catering contracts, catering pricing, dietary requirements, tasting sessions, catering coordination with venue (venue may have preferred caterers/restrictions). |
| **Florists** | Floral design for weddings/events — bouquets, buttonholes, centrepieces, ceremony flowers, reception flowers, etc. | Florist sourcing, florist relationships, floral design concepts, floral budgets, floral contracts, floral coordination with design theme, seasonal flowers. |
| **Photographers / videographers** | Photography and videography for weddings/events — capturing the event | Photographer/videographer sourcing, photographer/videographer relationships, photography/videography styles, packages, contracts, coordination with event timeline, copyright (photos/videos — copyright remains with photographer unless assigned). |
| **Hair and makeup artists** | Hair and makeup for bride and wedding party | Hair/makeup artist sourcing, relationships, styles, trials, contracts, coordination with bridal styling, schedule. |
| **Musicians / entertainment** | Music and entertainment for weddings/events — ceremony music, reception music, DJs, bands, entertainers, etc. | Musician/entertainment sourcing, relationships, music styles, Equipment, contracts, licensing (music licensing — PPL/PRS? if playing music at events, music licensing may be required — venues often handle this, but check), coordination with event. |
| **Bakeries / cake makers** | Wedding cakes, desserts — cake design, baking, delivery | Cake maker sourcing, relationships, cake design, cake tasting, contracts, delivery, coordination with theme. |
| **Stationers / calligraphers** | Wedding stationery — invitations, save-the-dates, menus, place cards, signage, etc. | Stationery supplier sourcing, calligrapher sourcing, designs, printing, coordination with theme. |
| **Dress designers / bridal wear** | Bridal dresses, wedding attire — dress sourcing, alterations, etc. | Dress designer/bridal wear sourcing, dress fitting, alterations, coordination with styling. |
| **Marquee / tent / furniture hire** | Marquees, tents, furniture (tables, chairs, etc.), lighting, etc. — for outdoor/covered events | Marquee/furniture hire companies, equipment hire, coordination with venue/weather, contracts, delivery/setup. |
| **Transport / logistics** | Transport for wedding party, guests, etc. — luxury cars, coaches, etc. | Transport providers, coordination with schedule, guest transport, etc. |
| **Accommodation** | Accommodation for wedding party, guests (especially for destination weddings) | Hotel/accommodation relationships, blocks of rooms, coordination with venue. |
| **Cake table / favours / gifts** | Wedding favours, gifts, cake table displays, etc. | Supplier sourcing, coordination with theme. |

**Building and managing supplier relationships:**

- Research and identify quality suppliers in your area (and for destination weddings, at destination).
- Visit venues, meet suppliers, assess quality, styles, reliability, pricing.
- Build relationships — trusted supplier partners, preferred supplier list.
- Negotiate rates (where possible), professional relationships.
- Manage supplier contracts, bookings, coordination.
- Ensure suppliers understand the wedding/event requirements, timeline, design.
- Have backup plans for key suppliers (in case of supplier failure).

**For non-resident wedding planners:**

Your UK LTD should build a strong UK supplier network — venues, caterers, florists, photographers, musicians, etc. across the UK. For destination weddings, build networks at destination locations. Multi-currency banking (Wise/Revolut) for international suppliers, destination wedding costs in local currencies, and international couples paying in their currencies.

## Step 3: Client Contracts, Deposits and Liability

Wedding planning and event design businesses need proper client contracts — wedding planning agreements, deposits, cancellation policies, liability clauses, and supplier agreements.

**Key elements of client contracts:**

| Element | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| **Scope of services** | What planning/design/coordination services are provided — full planning, partial planning, day-of coordination, design services, etc. — describe services, deliverables, packages. |
| **Timeline and schedule** | Planning timeline, key dates, wedding date, milestones, deadlines, meetings, etc. |
| **Fees and payment** | Fees for services — planning fee, design fee, coordination fee, etc. Payment terms — deposit, instalments, final payment, payment schedule, payment methods, currency. |
| **Deposit** | Deposit amount, when due, non-refundable portion (if any), deposit protection (if applicable). Deposits are important for wedding planners — securing dates, committing resources. |
| **Cancellation and postponement** | Cancellation policy — if client cancels, what happens to deposit/fees, any cancellation charges, etc. Postponement policy — if wedding date changes, rescheduling, additional fees, etc. Force majeure. |
| **Liability and limitations** | Limitations of liability — planner's liability for event issues, supplier failure, etc. Clear limits on liability. Insurance. |
| **Client responsibilities** | What the client is responsible for — providing information, making decisions, approvals, payments, etc. |
| **Supplier arrangements** | How suppliers are arranged — planner sources suppliers, client may choose suppliers, planner recommends suppliers, supplier contracts (planner may act as agent for client in booking suppliers). Clarify who contracts with suppliers, who pays suppliers, etc. |
| **Design and approval process** | Design concepts, approvals, revisions, client approval process. |
| **Relationship with suppliers** | Clarify planner's relationship with suppliers — planner acts as agent for client? Planner contracts with suppliers on behalf of client? Client contracts directly with suppliers? Affects liability, payments, etc. |
| **Confidentiality** | Confidentiality of client information, wedding details, etc. |
| **Data protection (GDPR)** | Handling client data (couple's data, family data, etc.) — GDPR compliance, privacy, data processing. |
| **Force majeure** | Force majeure — unforeseen events, events beyond control (e.g., extreme weather, venue closure, pandemics, supplier failure beyond planner's control, etc.) — cancellation/postponement due to force majeure. |
| **Dispute resolution** | How disputes are resolved — negotiation, mediation, etc. |

**Deposits and cancellation considerations:**

- Wedding planning involves significant deposits — securing the wedding date, committing planner time/resources.
- Deposits should be clear — amount, when due, what it covers, non-refundable elements (if any), refundability.
- Cancellation policies — protect the planner's time and resources if client cancels.
- Postponement — if wedding date changes, additional fees may apply for re-booking, re-planning.
- Consider wedding cancellation insurance for clients (or recommend it) — clients may want insurance for wedding cancellation, illness, etc.

**For non-resident wedding planners:**

Your UK LTD should have proper client contracts — wedding planning agreements, deposits, cancellation policies, liability clauses, supplier agreements. Use professional contracts — get legal advice if needed. Multi-currency banking (Wise/Revolut) for international couples and payments in various currencies.

## Step 4: Insurance for Wedding Planning & Event Design

Insurance is important for wedding planning and event design businesses — protecting against liability, event issues, professional negligence, equipment, and other risks.

**Key insurance types:**

| Insurance | Coverage | Why needed |
|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Public liability insurance** | Injury or damage to third parties — guests, public, at wedding venues, events, etc. | Essential for events — protects against injuries, property damage at venues/events. Many venues require public liability insurance from vendors/planners. |
| **Professional indemnity insurance (PI)** | Professional negligence, errors, omissions — bad advice, planning errors, failure to deliver, breach of contract, etc. | PI for wedding planners/event designers — protects against professional negligence claims. |
| **Equipment insurance** | Equipment — planning equipment, laptops, cameras (if using), design equipment, etc. | For equipment used in business. |
| **Cancellation insurance (for the event/wedding)** | Wedding cancellation insurance — for the couple (may be recommended to clients) — covers wedding cancellation due to illness, venue issues, etc. | Not a business insurance per se, but planners may recommend/arrange wedding cancellation insurance for clients. (Planner's own insurance doesn't cover client's wedding cancellation — that's the client's insurance.) |
| **Employers' liability insurance** | LEGAL REQUIREMENT if you employ staff (assistants, coordinators, etc.) — employee injury/illness at work | Required if employing. |
| **Contents/office insurance** | Office, contents, equipment | For office/contents. |
| **Cyber insurance** | Data breaches, cyberattacks — if holding client data, wedding details, etc. | For client data, GDPR. |
| **Motor insurance** | If using vehicles for business (traveling to venues, supplier meetings, etc.) — appropriate motor insurance (business use) | Business vehicle use — motor insurance with business use. |
| **Travel insurance** | For business travel — planner's travel for venue sourcing, supplier meetings, destination weddings, etc. | For planner's business travel. (Also recommend travel insurance for clients for destination weddings.) |

**Venue requirements:**

- Many wedding venues require vendors/planners to have public liability insurance (and sometimes specific coverage levels) as a condition of working at the venue.
- Check venue requirements — insurance certificates, coverage levels, etc.

**For non-resident wedding planners:**

Your UK LTD needs proper insurance — public liability (essential for events), professional indemnity, equipment, employers' liability (if employing), contents/office, cyber, motor (if vehicles), travel. Get quotes from insurers specialising in wedding/event industry insurance. Multi-currency banking (Wise/Revolut) for international couples and destination wedding costs in various currencies.

## Step 5: VAT on Wedding Planning & Event Design Services

VAT on wedding planning and event design services is generally straightforward — planning, coordination, design, styling services are generally standard-rated.

| Service | VAT treatment | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| **Wedding planning services** (full-service, partial, coordination) | Generally standard-rated for VAT (20%) | Wedding planning services — standard-rated. |
| **Event design services** | Generally standard-rated for VAT (20%) | Event design — standard-rated. |
| **Bridal styling services** | Generally standard-rated for VAT (20%) | Bridal styling — standard-rated. |
| **Venue sourcing / venue consultancy** | Generally standard-rated for VAT (20%) | Venue consultancy — standard-rated. |
| **Online wedding planning services / digital products** | Generally standard-rated for VAT (20%) | Digital planning services/products — standard-rated. |
| **Wedding fair/show ticket sales** | Generally standard-rated for VAT (20%) | Event tickets — standard-rated (though some admission to entertainment/meetings may be exempt — but wedding fair tickets generally standard-rated). |
| **If also selling goods (wedding items, accessories, etc.)** | Standard-rated for VAT (20%) (general goods) | Sale of goods — standard-rated (unless zero-rated items). |
| **Commission from suppliers (if planner takes commission from suppliers)** | May be standard-rated or outside scope depending on arrangement | If planner receives commission from suppliers for recommending/booking them — VAT treatment depends on whether it's a separate supply or part of the planning service. Generally, commission is standard-rated or outside scope depending on the setup. Get advice. |

**VAT registration:**

- **Mandatory** — over £90,000 rolling 12-month turnover — must register.
- **Voluntary** — below threshold, can register voluntarily — reclaim VAT on business costs (travel, supplier sample costs, marketing, professional fees, insurance, equipment, etc.) and appear more established.

**For non-resident wedding planners:**

Your UK LTD, if VAT-registered, charges VAT on wedding planning, event design, bridal styling, coordination services (standard rate 20%). For destination weddings abroad, the VAT treatment may differ (services supplied abroad may be outside UK VAT — place of supply rules). Multi-currency banking (Wise/Revolut) for international couples and destination wedding costs in various currencies. Get VAT advice for destination weddings and international services.
