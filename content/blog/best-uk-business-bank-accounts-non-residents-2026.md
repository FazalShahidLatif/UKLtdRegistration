## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a non-resident open a UK business bank account?

Yes — but it depends on the bank. Wise Business and Revolut Business allow remote onboarding from 170-200+ countries with biometric identity verification. Traditional UK banks (Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds, NatWest) typically require at least one UK-resident director and often a physical branch visit. Tide usually requires UK residency.

### What's the easiest UK business bank account for non-residents?

**Wise Business** is generally the easiest — open entirely online from 170+ countries with biometric ID verification, typically approved in 1-3 days. Revolut Business is a close second with similar remote onboarding.

### Do I need a UK address to open a business bank account?

For Wise and Revolut: no — you can use your home country address for identity verification and your UK LTD's registered office address for the business. For traditional banks and Tide: a UK address (and often UK residency) is usually required.

### Is my money safe in Wise or Revolut?

Wise and Revolut are fintechs, not traditional banks. Funds are held in safeguarded accounts (segregated from company funds) rather than FSCS-protected accounts. This means if the company fails, your funds should be protected, but they're not covered by the UK Financial Services Compensation Scheme (£85,000 protection). For most small business amounts, this is a reasonable trade-off for the accessibility and features.

### Can I get a UK business bank account for forex trading?

Forex trading businesses are considered high-risk by most banks and fintechs. Some platforms restrict or avoid forex-related businesses. Read our guide on [UK Bank Accounts for High-Risk Industries](/blog/uk-bank-accounts-high-risk-industries-gemstones-crypto-precious-metals-2026) for detailed guidance on which platforms may accept forex trading businesses.

### What documents do I need to open a UK business bank account as a non-resident?

Typically: proof of identity (passport), proof of address (utility bill or bank statement from your home country), company formation documents (Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association), and sometimes proof of business activity (website, contracts, invoices). Wise and Revolut do most verification through their apps via biometric ID + document upload.

### Can I open both Wise and Revolut for the same UK LTD?

Yes — many non-resident founders open both Wise Business and Revolut Business for the same UK LTD. This gives you redundancy, flexibility, and the different strengths of each platform. There's no restriction on having multiple business accounts for the same company.
