Registering online saves you time and avoids the need to visit Companies House or hire an agent in-person.

## Limited Company Online Formation: Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Check Company Name Availability

Before you set up, check your company name against the Companies House register.

**How to check:**
1. Go to [Companies House](https://find-and-apply.company-registration.service.gov.uk/)
2. Use the free company name search tool
3. Or use our [free company name check tool](https://ukltdregistration.com/company-name-check) — results in 10 seconds

> Our tool also suggests alternative names if your first choice is taken.

---

### Step 2: Prepare Your Company Details

You'll need:

| Detail | Example |
|--------|---------|
| **Company name** | "MYECOMMERCE UK LTD" |
| **Registered office address** | London or your preferred UK city |
| **SIC codes** | 62010 / 47812 (e-commerce) |
| **Director name, DOB, occupation** | Your details |
| **Shareholder details** | Your details (can be the same as director) |
| **PSC (Person with Significant Control)** | You (if you own >25%) |
| **UK electronic mail address** | yourname@email.com |

---

### Step 3: File Your Incorporation Application

You can file online:

1. Go to [Companies House eFiling Service](https://www.gov.uk/file-company-information-teaching-residents)
2. Select "Incorporate a company"
3. Fill in the online form
4. Submit — Companies House will review and issue a certificate

**Cost:** £100 (filing fee)

## After Incorporation: What to Do Next

Once your UK Ltd company is formed, you must:

1. **Open a UK business bank account** — our recommended partners
2. **Register for Corporation Tax** — within 3 months of starting to trade
3. **Apply for a UTR number** — HMRC issues this automatically
4. **Register for VAT** — if turnover exceeds £90,000 (2026 threshold)
5. **File annual accounts** — with Companies House
6. **File annual confirmation statement** — with Companies House
