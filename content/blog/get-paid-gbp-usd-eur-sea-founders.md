# Getting Paid in GBP, USD and EUR from Southeast Asia using a UK Ltd

For freelancers, agency owners, and SaaS founders in Southeast Asia (SEA), the biggest "invisible tax" is the currency exchange. If you are collecting $\$5,000$ from a US client into a local bank in Vietnam or Indonesia, you aren't just losing money on the exchange rate—you are losing it to intermediary bank fees and slow processing times.

The solution is to move your **Collection Point** to the UK. By utilizing a UK Ltd, you create a financial buffer that allows you to hold funds in their native currency and convert them only when the rate is in your favor.

## The Step-by-Step Payment Workflow
Here is exactly how to set up a professional, low-leakage payment flow from your home in SEA.

### Step 1: Establish the Legal Anchor (UK Ltd)
You cannot open a professional business account or a Stripe UK account without a legal entity. 
- **The Setup:** Incorporate a UK Limited company. 
- **The Result:** You now have a Company Registration Number (CRN) and a Registered Office address, which are the "passports" to the global financial system.

### Step 2: Activate the Treasury (Wise Business)
Once incorporated, open a Wise Business account. This gives you a **Multi-Currency Account**.
- **GBP Details:** A UK sort code and account number.
- **USD Details:** A US routing number and account number.
- **EUR Details:** An IBAN.
Now, when a client in New York wants to pay in USD, they send it to your US account. No one is "converting" the money yet—it stays as USD.

### Step 3: The Collection Front-End (Stripe UK)
To accept credit cards or automated subscriptions, link your Wise details to **Stripe UK**.
- **The Flow:** Client pays $ightarrow$ Stripe collects $ightarrow$ Stripe deposits into Wise.
- **The Advantage:** Because Stripe and Wise are both operating in the same currency (e.g., USD), there is zero conversion loss during the transfer.

### Step 4: The Strategic Exit (Local Bank)
Now you have a balance of USD, GBP, and EUR sitting in your Wise account. You have two choices:
1. **Convert on Demand:** When the exchange rate is favorable, convert the funds to your local currency (e.g., THB, VND, SGD) and send them to your local bank.
2. **Spend Directly:** Use a Wise Business Debit card to pay for your software subscriptions (AWS, OpenAI, Zoom) directly from your USD balance, avoiding conversion fees entirely.

## Pro Tip: The "Currency Hedge" for SEA Founders
One of the biggest advantages of this setup is the ability to **hedge against local currency devaluation**. 
If your local currency is volatile (e.g., the Vietnamese Dong or Indonesian Rupiah), holding your profits in USD or GBP within your Wise account acts as a financial shield. You only convert what you need for living expenses, keeping your business capital in a stable "hard" currency.
