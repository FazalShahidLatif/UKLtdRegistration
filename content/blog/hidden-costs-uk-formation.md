## The Price You See Is Not the Price You Pay

UK company formation is a commoditised product with a visible sticker price and a long tail of ongoing obligations. The formation fee buys you one thing: the act of filing with Companies House.

Everything that follows is separate, recurring, and — for a company with a December year-end — falls due at roughly six-month intervals for the rest of its life.

This guide sets out what a UK Ltd genuinely costs, so you can compare providers on total cost of ownership rather than headline price.

## Part One: The Formation Fee Itself

Every formation includes one statutory cost that cannot be avoided:

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Companies House incorporation fee | **£50** (online) | Statutory. Paid to Companies House directly. |
| Companies House incorporation fee | **£71** (paper) | Never file by paper. |
| Formation agent service fee | £0–£119+ | Pure service margin. This is what varies. |

Companies House reduced the online fee to £50 in May 2023. If a provider is still quoting the old £50 figure as their "government fee included" while charging £71, or if their fee structure is unclear, ask.

## Part Two: First-Year Running Costs

These are the costs that surprise people. All are **recurring annually**, not one-off.

### Registered Office Address

Legally mandatory, every year. This is the single most common hidden cost.

| Option | Typical annual cost |
|---|---|
| Free zone only (Companies House) | £0, but unusable for most businesses |
| Agent-provided London address | £50–£150/yr |
| Prestigious W1/EC1 address | £100–£300/yr |
| Virtual office with mail forwarding | £150–£400/yr |

The free Companies House address cannot be used as a director's service address, and mail is not forwarded. It also appears on the public register. See our [registered office guide](/blog/uk-registered-office-address-requirements-2026) for what your address must satisfy.

### Director Service Address

Optional but strongly advisable if your home address is not in the UK or you want it off the public register. Usually bundled with the registered office, sometimes charged separately at £30–£60/yr.

### Accounting and Annual Accounts

A UK Ltd must file accounts at Companies House every year, whether dormant or trading.

| Requirement | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Dormant accounts (unlimited company) | £0 if self-filed |
| Dormant accounts filed by an accountant | £50–£150/yr |
| Trading accounts (unlimited, small company) | £300–£900/yr |
| Trading accounts (micro-entity, ≤£250k) | £250–£700/yr |
| Audit (rarely required) | £500+ |

Small companies claiming audit exemption must meet two of three thresholds — turnover ≤£10.2m, balance sheet ≤£5.1m, ≤50 employees — and state the exemption claim explicitly on the balance sheet.

### Corporation Tax Registration

Free. But if you are non-resident, forming a company that generates UK-sourced income usually requires ** HMRC registration for Corporation Tax** within 3 months of starting activity, and often **VAT registration** as well. Both are free to register, both carry penalties if late.

### Confirmation Statement (CS01)

Filed once every 12 months, confirming directors, PSC and registered office.

| Method | Cost |
|---|---|
| Online, self-filed | £0 |
| Via agent, standard | £30–£50/yr |
| Via agent, same-day | Higher |

## Part Three: Recurring Costs That Founders Miss

### Banking

Not a UK company cost per se, but the practical reality is that non-residents cannot open a traditional UK account. Wise Business, Revolut, Airwallex and Mercury are the realistic options, and their fees should be factored into your model.

### Director and Shareholder Costs

- **Multiple directors:** each additional director must be verified and recorded
- **ID verification under ECCTA:** required for every director and PSC from 2026. ACSPs may charge for verification.
- **Right to work / standard verification** may be required in addition

### Professional Fees

Some activities need a professional rather than an online form:

| Activity | When needed | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|
| Trademark application | Protecting a brand | £400–£1,500 |
| Domain + email | Ongoing | £15–£50/yr |
| Website with compliance content | Bank and supplier onboarding | £0–£3,000 |
| Commercial contract review | Larger agreements | £200–£1,500 |
| Company secretarial service | Ongoing compliance | £200–£1,200/yr |

## What Formation Providers Differ On

These are the levers a low-cost provider pulls.

**Free registered office.** Offer included "for the first year". Renewal is a separate, material expense.

**Excess services marked "optional".** Same-day incorporation is only necessary if you have a genuine deadline — a tender response, a marketplace cut-off, a grant window. Paying a premium for it otherwise is waste.

**Companies House fee marked "from".** Some providers quote "from £50" as their headline while excluding the government fee entirely. Check the fee structure before comparing.

**No ACSP-registered agent.** Under ECCTA, directors and PSC details must be verified by an ACSP. Non-UK-resident formation is not reliably available through the free Companies House service.

**VAT not included.** If the quote excludes VAT, add 20% before comparing.

## Checklist Before You Pay Anyone

- [ ] Is the Companies House fee included, and is it £50 not £71?
- [ ] Is VAT applicable to the service, and is it included?
- [ ] Is the registered office included for **year 1 only** or year 1 **and** renewal?
- [ ] Does the agent hold current **ACSP** registration?
- [ ] Is ACSP identity verification for every director and PSC included?
- [ ] What happens if Companies House rejects the filing — is resubmission free?
- [ ] Can you supply your own registered office, or is the provider's mandatory?
