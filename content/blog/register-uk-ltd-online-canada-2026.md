## Step-by-Step: Registering a UK LTD from Canada

### Step 1: Choose Your Company Name

Check name availability on the [Companies House register](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/). Your name must end in "Limited" or "Ltd" and cannot be identical to an existing registered name.

Canadian founders often choose names that work in both English and French markets — a subtle but powerful credibility signal in European dealings.

### Step 2: Prepare Your ACSP Identity Verification

Under the **ECCTA 2026 rules**, every director and person with significant control (PSC) must undergo identity verification through an Authorised Corporate Service Provider (ACSP). 

Canadian applicants need:
- **Valid Canadian passport** (preferred) or permanent resident card
- **Proof of Canadian address** (utility bill, bank statement — dated within 3 months)
- **Biometric verification** — done remotely via webcam or smartphone

The verification process takes **2-5 minutes** and is fully digital.

### Step 3: Decide on Directors and Shareholders

A UK LTD requires:
- **At least one director** (can be the same person as the shareholder)
- **At least one shareholder** (can be the same person as the director)
- A **Person with Significant Control (PSC)** — usually the same person

Canadian founders can be both director and sole shareholder. There is **no requirement for a UK-resident director**.

### Step 4: Choose Your Registered Office Address

Your UK LTD must have a registered office address in the UK. This is where Companies House and HMRC send official correspondence.

Options for Canadian founders:
- **London virtual office** — prestigious WC1, W1, or EC1 address from £99/year
- **Director's service address** — keeps your home address off the public record
- **Physical office** — if you have one in the UK (rare for remote founders)

> **Important:** You cannot use a PO Box as your registered office. It must be a physical address where someone can receive mail during business hours.

### Step 5: File Your Incorporation

Your ACSP provider (that's us) files the incorporation directly with Companies House online. The process takes:

- **Same day** for straightforward applications (most Canadian founders)
- **Up to 24 hours** on busy days
- **Up to 3-6 hours** typically

You'll receive:
- **Certificate of Incorporation** (PDF)
- **Company number** (unique 8-digit identifier)
- **Memorandum and Articles of Association**
- **Share certificates** (digital)

### Step 6: Register for Corporation Tax

Within 3 months of starting business activity, you must register for Corporation Tax with HMRC. Your UK LTD will receive a **Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR)** by post within 14 days.

Canadian founders should also consider:
- **VAT registration** if your taxable turnover exceeds £90,000 (2026 threshold)
- **PAYE registration** if you plan to hire UK-based employees
- **Dividend strategy** — UK LTDs pay Corporation Tax on profits, then dividends are paid from post-tax profits

## Tax Considerations for Canadian Founders

### Corporation Tax

UK LTDs pay **Corporation Tax** on profits:
- **19%** on profits up to £50,000 (small profits rate, 2026)
- **25%** on profits above £250,000 (main rate, 2026)
- **Marginal relief** between £50,000 and £250,000

Canadian founders operating remotely often fall into the small profits rate in their first years.

### Canada-UK Double Taxation Agreement

The [Canada-UK Tax Treaty](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/foreign-income/foreign-tax-credits/online-services/non-resident-tax-and-withholding-agreements-tax-treaties.html) prevents double taxation on the same income. Key points:

- **Dividends** paid from a UK LTD to a Canadian resident are typically subject to a **withholding tax** (usually 5-15% depending on the structure)
- **Business profits** are generally taxable only in the country where the company is managed and controlled
- **Canadian founders** should consult a cross-border tax professional to understand their personal tax obligations

### "Central Management and Control" Risk

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) may treat a UK LTD as a **Canadian tax resident** if its "central management and control" is exercised from Canada. This is a complex area — if all your directors make decisions from Toronto or Vancouver, the CRA could argue the company is Canadian for tax purposes.

**Mitigation strategies:**
- Hold board meetings outside Canada when possible
- Document decision-making processes clearly
- Consider having at least one director based outside Canada
- Seek professional cross-border tax advice

## Common Questions from Canadian Founders

### Do I need to live in the UK to register a UK LTD?

No. You can register and run a UK LTD 100% remotely from Canada. There is no requirement for any director to reside in the UK.

### Can I use my Canadian address for the company?

Your company must have a UK registered office address. However, you can use a **director's service address** (provided by your formation agent) to keep your Canadian home address off the public Companies House record.

### Will UK banks accept a Canadian director?

Traditional UK banks (Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds) typically require at least one UK-resident director for business accounts. However, **Wise, Revolut, and Airwallex** accept Canadian directors with remote verification — and they provide real UK GBP account details.

### How long does it take to form a UK LTD from Canada?

Most applications are approved within **3-6 hours** via Companies House online filing. On busy days it can take up to 24 hours. Your ACSP verification is completed in minutes.

### Do I need to pay Canadian tax on my UK LTD's profits?

This depends on your specific situation. The Canada-UK Tax Treaty and CRA rules around "central management and control" are complex. You should consult a cross-border tax professional. Many Canadian founders find that their UK LTD is tax-resident in the UK if managed appropriately.
