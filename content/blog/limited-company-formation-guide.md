# Limited Company Formation: Your Complete 2026 Guide to UK Ltd Setup

Limited company formation is the process of registering a UK limited company with Companies House. For 2026, the process is faster and more accessible than ever — you can form your UK Ltd company in 24 hours, from anywhere in the world, with a licensed formation agent.

---

## What Is Limited Company Formation?

Limited company formation is the legal process of registering a company as a limited liability entity with Companies House, the UK's registrar of companies.

When you register a limited company, the company becomes a separate legal entity from its directors and shareholders. This means:

- **Limited liability** — your personal assets are protected if the company goes into debt
- **Separate legal identity** — the company can own assets, sign contracts, and sue/be sued
- **Corporate tax** — the company pays Corporation Tax on its profits (not personal income tax)
- **Perpetual succession** — the company continues to exist even if directors/shareholders change

**Limited company formation vs. sole trader:**
- Sole trader: simpler, no Companies House registration, you're personally liable
- Limited company: more paperwork, but protects personal assets and looks more professional

## How to Start a Business in the UK

Starting a business in the UK in 2026 follows one of three routes, and the right choice depends on your liability, tax, and growth plans:

| Route | Best for | Liability | Registration |
|-------|----------|-----------|--------------|
| **Sole trader** | Testing an idea, low turnover | Unlimited personal liability | Self Assessment with HMRC |
| **Limited company (Ltd)** | Most founders — protection + credibility | Limited to company assets | Companies House incorporation |
| **Partnership / LLP** | Two or more founders, professional services | Varies by structure | Companies House or HMRC |

For most founders — especially non-residents and online businesses — the limited company is the default choice because it separates your personal finances from the business and is the structure UK banks and payment providers expect.

### How Do You Register a Business in the UK?

Registering a business in the UK means incorporating a limited company at Companies House. You cannot register a limited company name without incorporating it — the name is approved as part of the filing. The steps are:

1. Choose and check your company name
2. Appoint a UK registered office address
3. Appoint at least one director and one shareholder
4. Prepare your Articles of Association
5. File the IN01 incorporation form
6. Register for Corporation Tax, VAT, and PAYE
7. Open a UK business bank account

Our same-day formation service handles steps 1–5 and guides you through 6–7.

### What Are the Requirements for a Limited Company?

The requirements for a UK limited company are set out in the [requirements table above](#limited-company-formation-requirements): at least one director aged 18+, at least one shareholder, a UK registered office address, a unique name, at least one SIC code, PSC details, and ACSP identity verification for all directors and PSCs. There is no minimum share capital and no UK residency requirement.

### What Are the Requirements for a Corporation?

In the US, "corporation" is the standard term for any registered company. In the UK, the equivalent is a **limited company** registered at Companies House, so the requirements for a corporation and the requirements for a UK limited company are the same seven things:

1. At least one director aged 18 or over
2. At least one shareholder
3. A UK registered office address (physical, not a P.O. box)
4. A unique name that passes Companies House checks
5. At least one SIC code
6. Details of every Person with Significant Control (PSC)
7. ACSP identity verification for all directors and PSCs under ECCTA 2026

There is no minimum share capital and no UK residency requirement.

## How to Create a Business from Home

You do not need commercial premises to register a company. The registered office address is a legal address for official mail — it is not your workplace. To create a business from home:

* **Use a registered office service** so your home address stays off the public register.
* **Keep records digitally** — accounting records must be kept, but they do not need to be held at a physical office.
* **Claim allowable expenses** — a proportion of utility and internet costs may be claimable if you work from home, subject to HMRC rules.
* **Check your lease or mortgage** — most residential tenancies allow a home business; some require landlord consent.

Freelancers, consultants, e-commerce sellers, software developers, and online coaches all routinely trade from home under a UK limited company.

## The Limited Company Formation Process (7 Steps)

### Step 1: Choose a Company Name

Your company name must:

- Be unique (no other Company House registration)
- Not contain restricted words (unless licensed)
- End with "Limited" or "Ltd" (or "Public Limited Company" / "PLC" for public companies)
- Not be offensive

**Check availability** at [Companies House](https://find-and-apply.company-registration.service.gov.uk/) or with our [free company name check tool](https://ukltdregistration.com/company-name-check).

---

### Step 2: Appoint a Registered Office Address

You need a UK registered office address. This is where Companies House and HMRC will send official mail.

- **London registered office** — from £49.99/yr
- **Manchester registered office** — from £49.99/yr
- **Virtual office with mail handling** — from £49.99/yr

### Step 3: Appoint Directors and Shareholders

| Role | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| **Director** | 18+, must consent to appointment, must provide ID |
| **Shareholder** | Can be 1 person (can be the same as director) |
| **PSC** | Anyone with >25% voting rights (must be disclosed) |

---

### Step 4: Prepare Your Articles of Association

The articles are the company's constitution — the rules for how it's run.

- **Model articles** (default) — the standard rules used by most UK Ltd companies
- **Custom articles** — if you have specific needs (e.g., multiple classes of shares, non-resident board members)

---

### Step 5: File the Incorporation Form

The incorporation application (IN01) requires:

- Company name (unique)
- Registered office address
- SIC codes (what the business does)
- Director details (name, DOB, country of residence, occupation)
- Shareholder details
- PSC details

**File online** at Companies House — or let us handle it.

---

### Step 6: Register for Taxes

After incorporation, register for:

1. **Corporation Tax** — within 3 months of starting to trade
2. **VAT** — if turnover exceeds £90,000 (2026 threshold)
3. **PAYE** — if you employ staff

---

### Step 7: Open a UK Business Bank Account

Apply for a UK business bank account with:

- Company incorporation certificate
- Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR)
- Bank account opening documents

## Common Limited Company Formation Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not checking the name** — choosing a name already taken
2. **Wrong SIC codes** — misrepresenting what the business does
3. **Missing PSC details** — failure to identify people with >25% voting rights
4. **Forgetting the registered office** — Companies House will issue fines
5. **Leaving the registered office address public** — using a corporate service
6. **Missing annual filings** — late confirmation statement or accounts
