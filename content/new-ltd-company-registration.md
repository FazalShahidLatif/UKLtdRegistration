# New LTD Company Registration in the UK

Registering a new LTD company in the UK is fast, secure, and increasingly digital. This guide covers step-by-step requirements, expected timelines, costs, and how to check name availability before you file with Companies House.

## Quick overview
- Typical Companies House filing fee: £12–£100 (depending on service)
- Usual processing time: same-day to 24 hours (online agents)
- Key documents: director details, registered office, SIC code, articles of association

## Step-by-step: How to register a new LTD
1. Check company name availability (see our guide below).
2. Choose directors and company officers.
3. Decide on a registered office address.
4. Select SIC codes that describe your business.
5. File incorporation with Companies House (online or via agent).
6. Obtain your Certificate of Incorporation and set up business banking.

## Costs and optional services
We offer free basic formation using electronic filings or same-day priority packages for faster processing. Expect additional costs for service addresses, ACSP identity verification (required for some jurisdictions), and expedited banking assistance.

## Common Questions
### How long does it take to register?
Most online registrations complete within 24 hours; priority same-day services are available for an additional fee.

### Can a non-resident register a UK LTD?
Yes. Non-residents can incorporate a UK company, but you'll need a UK service address and may require additional identity verification for banking.
