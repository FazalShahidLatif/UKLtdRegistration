# LTD Company UK Registration

Complete guide to registering an LTD company in the UK. Learn the legal requirements, director responsibilities, and practical steps to file accurately with Companies House.

## Who can register
- UK residents and non-residents
- Sole founders or multiple shareholders
- Businesses of all sizes looking for limited liability protection

## Key compliance points
- Registered office address must be a UK address and is publicly visible unless a service address is used.
- Directors must provide verified identity information under new 2026 rules.
- Annual filings include Confirmation Statement and Accounts.
