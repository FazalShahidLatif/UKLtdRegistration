# Check Company Name Availability UK

Before you register, confirm your proposed name is available and compliant with Companies House rules. This page explains how to search, common name restrictions, and trademark checks.

## How to check availability
- Use the Companies House beta name availability search.
- Search existing trademarks via the UK IPO.
- Avoid restricted or offensive words; consult our list of banned terms.

## Practical tips
- Choose a distinct name to avoid trademark disputes.
- Include a geographic or descriptive modifier if your primary name is taken.
- Reserve multiple variations to protect brand identity.
